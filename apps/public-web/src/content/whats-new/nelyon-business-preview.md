Nelyon Business brings together tools for a commercial presence inside the Nelyon ecosystem. This is an **editorial preview** of the experience, not an announcement of public or universal availability.

## One connected workspace

The workspace includes a product catalog, orders, media, Ads campaigns and an Analytics view. Finance and Team remain inside the private application and require authorized access.

## Explore the experience

See the overview on the [Nelyon Business page](/business). Access to private tools requires authentication and permissions.
