## Decisions blocking activation

- Owner/legal: legal entity and trade name, jurisdiction, registered/business address and verified support, privacy, legal and copyright contacts.
- Owner/legal: age and guardian-consent policy; governing law, forum/arbitration, liability and dispute language; content license and moderation/termination notice; exact payment, BDAG, fees, refunds, settlement and payout commitments.
- Product/operations/legal: retention by category, deletion/export process and exclusions for financial, order and audit records; actual provider processing, regions, transfer disclosures, security language and international handling.
- Editorial/legal: final wording, reviewed translations and available locales, final versions, effective dates and date-change process; final Privacy and Terms approvals.

Do not flip the manifest to `approved` as an activation shortcut. A later authorized phase must reconcile the wording, set verified identity/contact values, replace the `draft-*` technical version with an approved one, record review evidence, verify production behavior and equivalent mobile/web rendering, then migrate visible routes. Community, Copyright, Monetization and Cookies remain outside this authority and require separate review. Existing moderation `terms#` references must be mapped by reviewed meaning and version before retiring any legacy section.
